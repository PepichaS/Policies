## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. My notifications are not showing up. What should I do?
Since *Just Commit* runs locally on your device, it relies on system permissions.
1. Go to your iPhone **Settings**.
2. Scroll down to find **Just Commit**.
3. Tap on **Notifications** and ensure the switch is **ON**.
4. Make sure you have set a specific time for the habit reminder in the app.

### 2. Where are my photos and data stored?
Your privacy is our priority. All habit data, streaks, and photo proofs are stored **locally on your device** (using internal storage). We do not upload your photos to any external cloud server.
*Note: If you delete the app, your data will be lost unless you have an iCloud device backup.*

### 3. I cannot add a photo proof.
Please ensure you have granted the app permission to access the Camera.
* Go to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera** and enable access for *Just Commit*.

### 4. How do I delete a habit?
To delete a habit:
1. Open the specific Habit Details screen.
2. Look for the delete icon (top right).
*Warning: This action cannot be undone.*
